🔺 What Is the Illuminati? 🔺

Explore the ideas, history and modern narratives surrounding the name Illuminati.

The Illuminati

Modern Illuminati narratives describe an elite or private organization bringing together influential individuals across political, financial, cultural and geographical boundaries.

These narratives describe a collective concerned with the prosperity and future of humanity, while emphasizing privacy and anonymity.

The modern claims presented here should be understood as beliefs or organizational narratives rather than independently verified facts.

Visitors are encouraged to examine information carefully, ask questions and make their own informed decisions.

"The Pyramid, the Eye, the Light, the Eternal Circle."

Global Unity

The material provided for this website presents the idea of a brotherhood that seeks unity across political, religious, geographical and cultural boundaries.

Its stated purpose includes encouraging cooperation, personal development, humanitarian assistance and a better understanding between people.

Historical Origins

The historical Bavarian Illuminati belongs to the European Enlightenment of the eighteenth century.

1748

Johann Adam Weishaupt

Johann Adam Weishaupt was born in Ingolstadt, Bavaria. He later became a professor of canon law at the University of Ingolstadt.

1776

The Founding

Weishaupt founded the historical Illuminati in Ingolstadt on May 1, 1776. The group was connected with Enlightenment ideas concerning reason, education and social reform.

1780s

Suppression

The historical organization was eventually prohibited by the Bavarian authorities and declined during the 1780s.

Historical Context

The historical Bavarian Illuminati should be distinguished from modern internet claims and narratives about a continuing global organization.

Historical documentation establishes the eighteenth-century group; claims about a present-day worldwide organization are matters of modern belief and narrative rather than established historical fact.

Can I change my mind?
