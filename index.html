STAGE 03

Reflect

How Membership Works

Membership is voluntary. Review each stage before submitting an application.

STEP 01

Read the Information

Learn about the organization and understand what participation involves.

STEP 02

Submit Interest

Complete the membership interest form with accurate information and your reasons for wanting to join.

STEP 03

Orientation

Applicants who proceed can receive further information about community expectations and participation.

STEP 04

Review

Take time to review the rules, responsibilities, privacy information, and code of conduct.

STEP 05

Decision

Decide freely whether you want to continue. No applicant should feel pressured to participate.

STEP 06

Participation

Approved members can begin participating according to the organization's stated rules and community standards.

Our Principles

Clear principles help create a responsible and respectful community.

FREE CHOICE Participation should always be voluntary and free from coercion.
RESPECT Members should treat other people with dignity regardless of their background or beliefs.
PRIVACY Personal information should be handled responsibly and only used for legitimate organizational purposes.
TRANSPARENCY Important expectations, requirements, and costs should be clearly explained before anyone decides to participate.
RESPONSIBILITY Every participant is responsible for making informed decisions and respecting applicable laws and community rules.
