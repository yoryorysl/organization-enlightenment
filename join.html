Before You Apply

Make sure you understand the basic principles of responsible participation.

Is participation voluntary?

Yes. A person should make their own decision about whether to participate and should not be pressured into doing so.

Should I understand the information first?

Yes. Review the history, principles and enlightenment material before making a decision.

Can I ask questions?

Yes. Questions should be encouraged when a person needs clarification before proceeding.

Are harmful acts part of joining?

No. Participation should not require anyone to harm themselves, another person or an animal, or to commit an illegal act.

What happens after an application?

An application can be reviewed according to the applicable membership requirements. Further information can then be provided where appropriate.
