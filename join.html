How to Join

Membership begins with understanding. Follow the steps below and make an informed decision before submitting your interest.

Before You Apply

Make sure you understand the basic expectations.

18+ Recommended

Applicants should meet the organization's stated age requirements and applicable local laws.

Understand the Organization

Read the available educational material and understand what participation involves.

Join Voluntarily

Membership should be your own decision. You should never feel pressured to join.

The Membership Process

A simple step-by-step path from learning to participation.

1

Explore

Read the organization's introduction, philosophy, values, expectations, and educational information.

2

Learn

Complete the available enlightenment material and take time to understand the organization's principles.

3

Submit Your Interest

Provide the requested information through the official application or contact channel.

4

Application Review

Your application can be reviewed according to the organization's membership requirements and standards.

5

Orientation

Applicants who proceed can receive further information about community standards, responsibilities, and participation.

6

Membership Decision

After reviewing the information, you can freely decide whether you want to continue with membership.

Important: Never provide passwords, banking credentials, cryptocurrency wallet recovery phrases, or other highly sensitive information as part of an application. Any legitimate membership requirements should be clearly explained before participation.
