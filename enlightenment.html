The Path of Enlightenment

Enlightenment begins with learning. Explore the principles, philosophy, responsibilities, and expectations of the organization before deciding whether participation is right for you.

What Is Enlightenment?

A journey centered on knowledge, reflection, and personal development.

Knowledge Before Membership

Every prospective participant should have the opportunity to understand what an organization represents before making a decision to join.

Our enlightenment section provides introductory information about philosophy, values, community expectations, personal responsibility, and the importance of making informed choices.

Think for Yourself

Enlightenment should encourage thoughtful questions rather than require someone to accept information without examination.

Take your time, ask questions, evaluate information carefully, and make your own decision about whether you wish to continue.

Stages of Learning

Follow the educational path at your own pace.

STAGE 01

Introduction

Learn the basic history, purpose, terminology, and publicly available information about the organization.

STAGE 02

Philosophy

Explore the organization's stated beliefs, principles, values, educational ideas, and approach to personal development.

STAGE 03

Self-Reflection

Consider your personal goals, motivations, boundaries, and expectations before taking any further step.

STAGE 04

Questions

Review the frequently asked questions and seek clarification about anything you do not understand.

STAGE 05

Responsibilities

Understand the community standards, code of conduct, privacy practices, and responsibilities associated with participation.

STAGE 06

Informed Decision

After reviewing the available information, decide freely whether you want to continue toward the membership process.

Important: Participation should always be voluntary. No person should be pressured or threatened into joining, remaining a member, providing personal information, or making a payment.
